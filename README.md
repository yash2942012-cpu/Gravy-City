# Gravy-City

GRAVY CITY PIZZA: DO NOT LATE is a ridiculous 3D pizza delivery comedy.

You're the last driver with no vehicle, on the night shift but there is day, inside Greater Gravy City — a sprawling 400×400 procedurally generated city. 

Your job is simple: deliver 10 pizzas to 10 doors before your shift ends. Keep them hot to earn bigger tips and try to earn the coveted GRAVY CITY GOLD grade. 

The city, however, is doing everything in its power to stop you. Unionized pigeons will steal your pizzas, greasy roads will send you sliding, your mom will guilt-trip you 
over the phone, fake ads will hijack your screen, and too many wrong deliveries will have the police on your tail. 

Intentionally designed to make sure you are late.
